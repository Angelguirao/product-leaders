This project is an **unofficial** community atlas. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dani Diestre, Entrevistas a Product Leaders, guests, or their employers.

## Careful path

| Practice | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Link every card to the official episode (YouTube / Spotify / RSS) | Readers hear the original; we don’t replace it |
| Publish **original synthesis** of how companies operate product — not full transcripts | Transformative notes, not a parallel archive |
| Keep caption / Whisper extracts in local `raw/` (gitignored) | Avoid redistributing verbatim interview text at scale |
| Short quotes only, attributed | Fair-use style excerpting |
| No audio/video files in the repo | Platform ToS + copyright risk on media copies |

## Unclear / case-by-case

- Fair use depends on jurisdiction, quote length, and whether notes substitute for listening.
- Guests or hosts can ask for correction or removal of a specific card — honor reasonable requests.

**Not legal advice.** For commercial use, talk to a lawyer.

## Takedown

Rights holders: open an issue or contact the maintainer. Prefer fixing attribution and linking over adversarial process.
