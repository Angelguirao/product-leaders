# ApoloKids

> ApoloKids treats inclusion as a core product constraint for kids’ learning games — accessibility shapes scope, not a late compliance checklist.

- Stage: early
- Practices: enterprise, discovery, delivery, roles

## Andrea López Castillo

### Operating loop

- The development of Cocoro Kids games involves user research with professionals (teachers, psychologists, pedagogues) to identify key areas and objectives. This informs game design, focusing on language and communication, cognitive skills, and socio-emotional development. The process integrates business goals and trends, such as creating games around events like the Olympics, to ensure relevance and engagement.

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Product Manager, informed by user research with professionals and data analysis.
- **Prioritization:** Product Manager, with a focus on B2B needs for the Cocoro Schools platform.
- **Shipping:** Not explicitly detailed, but implied to be managed by the product team.
- **Sales Input:** B2B sales department is actively developing for the Cocoro Schools platform.
- **Design:** Designers collaborate with psychologists and pedagogues.

### Evidence

- *Simply by adding an option for reduced visual stimuli, it helps this child maintain more attention in a game.*
- *We want the content to be adapted to those children and for them to be able to play.*
