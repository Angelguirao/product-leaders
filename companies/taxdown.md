# Taxdown

> Taxdown sells continuous tax relief via subscription plus AI document handling, not a once-a-year filing tool.

- Stage: early
- Practices: enterprise, ai_product, discovery, delivery

## Alvaro Falcones

### Operating loop

- The company's operating loop involves:
- **User Input & Data Integration:** Gathering user information, often directly from tax authorities.
- **AI-Powered Assistance:** Utilizing AI copilots and assistants to help users understand their declarations and provide accurate responses.
- **Tax Calculation & Filing:** Performing complex tax calculations and facilitating the submission of declarations.
- **Continuous Support & Advice:** Offering ongoing guidance through a subscription model, enabling users to make informed financial decisions year-round.
- **Product Improvement:** Iteratively refining the platform based on user feedback and AI-driven insights.

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Primarily driven by understanding user pain points related to bureaucracy and taxes, with a focus on continuous feedback loops and AI analysis.
- **Prioritization:** Supported by a subscription model that allows for year-round user engagement, informing product development beyond the annual tax season.
- **Shipping:** Managed with a focus on speed and simplicity, exemplified by early hypotheses centered on user experience and rapid iteration.
- **Sales-Input:** Integrated through the subscription model and direct user interaction, with a growing B2B segment also contributing.
- **Design:** Emphasizes speed, simplicity, and user experience, with a unique approach of enabling fiscal experts to program tax logic.

### Evidence

- *The company's mission is no longer just taxes, but to liberate the world from bureaucracy.*
- *We have 4 million users, we have returned 400 million euros to people that are theirs.*
