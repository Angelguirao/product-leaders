# Capchase

> Capchase’s core IP is the SaaS underwriting engine — funding products expand from that risk model, not the other way around.

- Stage: growth
- Practices: discovery, delivery, org_shape, roles

## Ignacio Moreno (Guli)

### Operating loop

- The company's operating loop involves:
- 1.  Underwriting SaaS companies to assess risk and provide capital.
- 2.  Leveraging this underwriting capability to develop new financial products.
- 3.  Expanding horizontally by offering software tools that facilitate transactions and revenue lifecycle management.
- 4.  Using data and technology to refine underwriting and product offerings.

### Ownership

- **Discovery**: Primarily driven by product managers and the broader team's understanding of customer needs and market opportunities.
- **Prioritization**: Guided by company-wide OKRs, with product teams translating these into actionable product initiatives.
- **Shipping**: Teams work in squads with dedicated product managers, designers, and engineers, focusing on delivering value quickly.
- **Sales Input**: Integrated into the product development process, particularly for new offerings like Capchase Pay.
- **Design**: Handled by product designers within squads, working closely with product managers and engineers.

### Evidence

- *The core of our business, the underwriting, is technology.*
- *We want to live in a world where a CRO uses Capchase for all their transactions.*
