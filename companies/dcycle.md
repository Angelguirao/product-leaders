# Dcycle

> Dcycle makes product teams talk to customers and ship without waiting for CEO/sales permission — autonomy is the operating system.

- Stage: enterprise
- Practices: enterprise, discovery, delivery, roles

## Juanjo Mestre

### Operating loop

- The company's operating loop emphasizes autonomous teams making decisions based on customer feedback and data. This involves engineers directly engaging with customers to understand problems and propose solutions, moving away from traditional backlog-driven development. The focus is on enabling teams to "invent their own solutions" and take ownership of the entire process from discovery to delivery.

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Product teams, with engineers directly interacting with customers.
- **Prioritization:** Product teams, driven by customer problems and business metrics.
- **Shipping:** Product teams, with an emphasis on autonomous delivery.
- **Sales Input:** Close collaboration with sales and marketing, especially for B2B/Enterprise clients.
- **Design:** Integrated within product teams, with product designers often taking on dual roles.

### Evidence

- *The best way to screw up was to listen to the CEO.*
- *The best data is the data that isn't asked for.*
