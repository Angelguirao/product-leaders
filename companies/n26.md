# N26

> N26’s product is the branch: one global banking platform, then ‘glocal’ local IBANs and market features so the app can be the primary account without physical branches.

- Stage: growth
- Practices: discovery, delivery, org_shape, roles

## Guido Lonetti

### Operating loop

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Global Platform Strategy] --> B{Local Market Adaptation};
    B --> C[Glocalization: Local IBANs & Marketing];
    C --> D[Primary Account User (PAU) Growth];
    D --> E[Iterative Product Development];
    E --> A;
```

### Ownership

- Discovery: Product teams, supported by local market insights.
- Prioritization: Each team has autonomy over its prioritization practices.
- Shipping: Product teams manage their own shipping processes.
- Sales-input: Local market teams provide input.
- Design: Integral to the product development process, with a dedicated UX library.

### Evidence

- *Nine out of 10 millennials prefer going to the dentist before going to their bank branch.*
- *We aim to have a global platform structure but to focus strongly locally.*
