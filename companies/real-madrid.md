## Carlos Sánchez

Carlos Sánchez, Head of Product at Real Madrid, discusses the unique challenges and opportunities of building digital products for a massive global fanbase. He highlights the shift from a traditional sports club model to an entertainment company, emphasizing the importance of digital products in this evolution.

### Key moves

- **Unified User Identification:** Implemented a single sign-on system to manage diverse user types (socios, fans, premium subscribers, employees), simplifying access and personalization.
- **Fan-Centric Value Delivery:** Focuses on providing value to the broad fan base, understanding that growth and monetization opportunities lie primarily with this segment, while still serving socios.
- **Digital-Physical Integration:** Leveraging the new stadium to create enhanced physical experiences through digital layers, blurring the lines between online and offline engagement.
- **Content Beyond Live Matches:** Developing products and content around live football, acknowledging limitations on broadcasting rights and catering to diverse fan consumption habits (highlights, behind-the-scenes).
