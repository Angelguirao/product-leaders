# Nominis

> Nominis teaches kids English like Netflix — interactive stories and gamification replace classroom drills for ages 6–12.

- Stage: early
- Practices: ai_product, discovery, delivery, metrics

## Rodrigo Servert

### Operating loop

- The core of Nominis's offering is interactive stories, similar to a video game, where children make choices that affect the narrative. These stories are supplemented with integrated, contextualized exercises for speaking, listening, and vocabulary. The platform also includes mini-games and a gamification layer with collectible creatures called Nomis, which evolve alongside the child's language proficiency. New story chapters are released weekly, creating an ongoing narrative similar to a streaming service.

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Primarily driven by user feedback, particularly from parents canceling subscriptions, and engagement metrics. The team also uses beta testers and feedback from schools.
- **Prioritization:** Defined by the CEO, Rodrigo Servert, who also handles product definition and design. OKRs are set, focusing on problems to solve rather than specific solutions, with input from the tech team.
- **Shipping:** Managed through a bi-weekly sprint planning process, with the CEO passing "missions" to the tech team for evaluation and execution.
- **Sales Input:** While the primary customer is the parent, the child is the end-user. This dynamic influences product design and marketing.
- **Design:** Handled by the CEO, who creates mockups and defines product strategy.

### Evidence

- *We want to become the Disney for learning languages.*
- *The child is completely free, independent, and autonomous when using the product.*
