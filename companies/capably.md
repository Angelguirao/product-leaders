# Capably

> Capably sells enterprise AI adoption as data prep + governance + ops integration — the product delegates working hours to AI once the company data is ready.

- Stage: enterprise
- Practices: enterprise, ai_product, discovery, delivery

## Rafa Pulido

### Operating loop

- The core of Capably.ai's platform involves:
- 1.  **Data Preparation:** Ensuring company data is ready for AI and LLM consumption.
- 2.  **Governance & Safety:** Implementing controls for security, safety, and ethical AI use.
- 3.  **AI Employee Integration:** Creating and deploying AI agents that integrate into existing workflows.
- 4.  **Process Delegation:** AI agents take over operational tasks, freeing up human working hours.
- 5.  **Continuous Learning:** AI employees receive feedback and learn to improve performance.

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Supported by the platform's ability to analyze user prompts and identify areas of high demand for AI delegation across departments.
- **Prioritization:** Driven by the company's strategic decision to focus on enabling AI adoption and delegating working hours, rather than solely on specific use cases.
- **Shipping:** The platform facilitates the integration of AI into existing workflows, making it seamless for users.
- **Sales Input:** The platform analyzes user prompts and interactions to understand company needs and areas for AI application.
- **Design:** The platform offers customizable AI employee avatars and names, allowing for a more human-like interaction to ease adoption.

### Evidence

- *The majority of companies say we do not allow AI.*
- *The reality is that many jobs are not that value-added.*
