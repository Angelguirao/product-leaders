# Spotify

- Stage: scale
- Practices: ai_product, discovery, delivery, org_shape

## Javier Moscardó

### Operating loop

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[User Feedback & Market Trends] --> B{Hypothesis Generation};
```

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Primarily owned by VPs and their teams, with a focus on understanding user needs and market shifts.
- **Prioritization:** Driven by strategic goals set at the VP level, balancing innovation with user experience.
- **Shipping:** Teams are empowered to ship, but with an emphasis on a cohesive user experience, suggesting centralized oversight for major changes.
- **Sales-input:** Not explicitly detailed, but likely integrated through business and content leadership.
- **Design:** Owned by the VP of Experience, with significant autonomy to define user interface and experience strategies.
