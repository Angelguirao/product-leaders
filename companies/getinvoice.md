# GetInvoice

> GetInvoice is a solo-founder AI maker loop: automate invoice pain for SMBs with AI tooling instead of hiring a classic product org.

- Stage: growth
- Practices: ai_product, discovery, delivery, growth

## AI con Miquel Palet

### Operating loop

- The core loop involves connecting email accounts (Gmail, Outlook) or portals (Amazon, Google Ads) to automatically scan for and extract invoice data. This data is then processed to identify key information like amounts, providers, and VAT, and can be automatically sent to accounting software (ERPs) or stored in cloud storage like Google Drive. The product also offers a Chrome extension for easier extraction from specific portals and provides insights into spending and subscription renewals.

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Primarily driven by direct user feedback via email and platform chat, with some use of session recording tools like Postw.
- **Prioritization:** Based on user requests and identified needs, with a focus on iterative development rather than formal PRDs or Figma designs.
- **Shipping:** Handled by the founder, with an emphasis on rapid iteration and getting an MVP to market quickly.
- **Sales Input:** Direct user interaction and feedback loop.
- **Design:** Intuitive, functional design developed iteratively by the founder, prioritizing usability over extensive design processes.

### Evidence

- *When you see someone using your product, there's nothing more precious than that.*
- *The core was invoice capture through email, and then adding support for other email types and portals.*
