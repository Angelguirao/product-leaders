# Decathlon

> Decathlon Outdoor uses the app to reposition from selling gear to enabling outdoor experiences — product marketing owns lifestyle, not only SKU pages.

- Stage: enterprise
- Practices: discovery, delivery, org_shape, roles

## Carlota Güell

### Operating loop

- User creates or finds a route.
- GPS guides user on the route.
- App tracks activity (distance, time).
- User earns loyalty points.
- Data informs product development and store strategy.

### Ownership

- Discovery: Product Managers, Product Designers, Engineers, PMM.
- Prioritization: Head of Product, PMM.
- Shipping: Product Managers, Engineers.
- Sales Input: Unknown (likely PMM and Marketing team).
- Design: Product Designers, Engineers.

### Evidence

- *The objective of developing applications that encourage going outside and practicing sports is also a way for Decathlon to say we are not just a sports product provider, but we also offer sports experiences that accompany you daily.*
- *We use tools like Amplitude to see all the actions a user takes in our app and can immediately see what isn't working.*
