# OnTrack

> OnTrack validated empty-truck logistics with a Telegram MVP first — problem (40% empty km) before process; tools stay ugly until the market answers.

- Stage: early
- Practices: discovery, delivery, founder

## Javier Escribano

### Operating loop

- Identify a core problem (e.g., 40% of truck kilometers are empty).
- Conduct focused research and validation (e.g., call-calling drivers and companies).
- Build an MVP using accessible tools (e.g., Telegram group for OnTrack's initial operations).
- Iterate based on learnings and market feedback.
- Scale operations and product offerings.

### Ownership

- Discovery: Primarily driven by founders and product leaders, with a focus on identifying and validating core problems.
- Prioritization: Supported by data analysis and strategic alignment with business goals.
- Shipping: Emphasizes iterative delivery and learning from market feedback.
- Sales-input: Not explicitly detailed, but implied through understanding customer needs.
- Design: Integrated early in the process, as seen with the Telegram MVP concept.

### Evidence

- *The problem we wanted to solve was that 40% of the kilometers trucks travel are empty.*
- *In product, it's always about going to the problem we want to solve.*
