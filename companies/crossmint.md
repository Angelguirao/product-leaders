# Crossmint

> Crossmint bets on programmable money primitives as APIs — wallets, payments, tokens — so product teams ship crypto rails without becoming protocol experts.

- Stage: early
- Practices: ai_product, discovery, delivery, roles

## Founder

### Operating loop

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify Market Need for Programmable Economy] --> B[Develop Core API Primitives (Wallets, Payments, Tokens)];
    B --> C[Create Packaged Solutions (e.g., Bank-in-a-Box, AI Agent Tools)];
    C --> D[Onboard Businesses (Fintech, AI Agents, Loyalty Programs)];
    D --> E[Iterate on APIs based on Client Feedback and Market Trends];
    E --> F[Adapt Go-to-Market Strategy to Emerging Use Cases];
```

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Market trends, client needs, and emerging use cases drive product development.
- **Prioritization:** Focus on creating generalizable and durable API primitives while adapting go-to-market strategies to current market demands.
- **Shipping:** Product engineering team organized by product (e.g., wallets, payments), with product managers creating both primitives and packaged solutions.
- **Sales Input:** Go-to-market team is specialized by vertical (Fintech, AI, Commerce, Gaming, Loyalty) and includes sales, customer success engineers, and marketing.
- **Design:** Product managers are responsible for creating packaged solutions, with potential future specialization into Product Marketing Managers for specific verticals.

### Evidence

- *The insight we had four years ago is that humanity has been digitizing and organizing a lot of things... but where we haven't reached is the most basic layer, the one based on property rights.*
- *We simplify all those complexities and that any programmer can say, Hey, I want to create a user wallet, put $100 in it, now transfer 50 with a call to a JavaScript function.*
