# Meta

> Product marketing managers at Meta focus on deeply understanding customer needs and portfolio performance to drive product strategy and market success.

- Stage: growth
- Practices: discovery, delivery, roles, growth

## Daniel Núñez

### Operating loop

- The discussion highlights a continuous loop involving:
- Deep customer and portfolio understanding
- Identifying customer needs, pain points, and gaps
- Translating insights into product value propositions and features
- Developing and launching products
- Gathering ongoing feedback for iteration and growth
- Communicating product value and strategy to stakeholders

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Product Marketing Managers (PMMs) and Product Managers (PMs) are heavily involved, with PMMs often leading direct customer interaction and insight gathering.
- **Prioritization:** Primarily owned by Product Managers, informed by PMM insights and market analysis.
- **Shipping:** A collaborative effort involving Product, Engineering, and Design teams.
- **Sales Input:** PMMs work closely with sales teams to gather insights and support deal closures.
- **Design:** While not explicitly detailed, the involvement of creative and design teams is mentioned in the context of developing user experiences and advertising formats.

### Evidence

- *A PMM has to deeply know their portfolio, know who their top clients are, top churners, top decliners, top growers, know them by heart.*
- *For me, it's the platform with the most sophisticated advertising technology in the world.*
