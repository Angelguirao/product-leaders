# IskayPet

> At IskayPet, product methods bend to a traditional retail P&L — digital ships only when stores, supply, and category economics can absorb the change.

- Stage: growth
- Practices: ai_product, discovery, delivery, org_shape

## Daniel Lovazzano

### Operating loop

- **Backlog Control:** Gaining control of the product backlog from stakeholders who dictate specific features rather than problem statements.
- **Agency Management:** Transitioning from heavy reliance on external agencies to building internal expertise and control.
- **Omnichannel Integration:** Leveraging physical stores to enhance the online customer experience and vice versa.
- **Digital Transformation:** Establishing a dedicated internal team to drive digital initiatives and cultural change.
- **AI for Post-Sales:** Investigating AI applications for improving post-purchase customer service and support.

### Ownership

- **Discovery:** Primarily driven by stakeholders, with a challenge to shift towards problem-based collaboration.
- **Prioritization:** Heavily influenced by stakeholders, particularly those focused on revenue.
- **Shipping:** Largely managed by external logistics providers, with potential for in-house solutions leveraging store inventory.
- **Sales Input:** Significant input from business and operational teams, often accustomed to providing feature requests.
- **Design:** Unknown, but likely involves a mix of internal and external agency contributions.

### Evidence

- *The backlog struggle is a kind of power control.*
- *It is normal that the company's know-how should not be with an agency, it has to be internal.*
