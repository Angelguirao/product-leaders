## Thesis
Nova Talent builds a premium B2C professional community on top of B2B headhunting — curated horizontal connections are the product bet, not just placements.


## Context
Nova Talent operates a dual B2C and B2B model. The B2C side offers a community for top talent, emphasizing meaningful connections, while the B2B side provides headhunting services for companies seeking this talent. The company originated in Sweden and has expanded to Milan, Italy, and Spain, with growing hubs in London and other cities.

## Operating loop
The core operating loop for Nova Talent's community features revolves around curated connections and content consumption. Key elements include:
*   **Gravity:** Weekly curated one-on-one video calls connecting members with similar profiles.
*   **Threads:** A new feature designed to document and make searchable valuable community discussions, moving beyond the limitations of WhatsApp groups.
*   **Nova Points:** A gamified system rewarding member engagement, including invitations and content contributions, which can be redeemed for merchandise or donations.
*   **Events:** A variety of in-person and virtual events, from dinners with leaders to specialized activities like surf trips, fostering community and networking.
*   **Content Consumption:** Users can access a feed of posts, recommendations, and job opportunities, with personalized content delivery.

## Ownership
*   **Discovery:** Primarily driven by analyzing existing community interactions (e.g., WhatsApp group content) and validating ideas through no-code tools before full implementation.
*   **Prioritization:** Focused on features that directly impact key metrics like Monthly Active Users (MAU) and member engagement, with a strong emphasis on community experience.
*   **Shipping:** The product and engineering team, though small, is highly efficient, leveraging no-code tools for initial validation and rapid iteration.
*   **Sales Input:** The B2B headhunting service provides direct input on the types of talent companies are seeking, influencing the community's focus.
*   **Design:** Integrated into the product development process, with a dedicated product design team.
