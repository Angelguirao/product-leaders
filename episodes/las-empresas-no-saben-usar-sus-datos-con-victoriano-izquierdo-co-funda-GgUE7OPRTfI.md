## Thesis
Graphext enables non-technical users to explore and derive insights from complex data through an intuitive, no-code interface, bridging the gap between raw data and actionable business strategies.

## Context
Graphext is a SaaS product that helps companies understand and leverage their data. Founded by Victoriano Izquierdo and his long-time partner, the company has been operating for nearly eight years, focusing on making data exploration accessible to a broader audience beyond data scientists.

## Operating loop
The core of Graphext's operating loop involves:
1.  **Data Ingestion**: Connecting to various data sources (CSV, Google Sheets, databases, etc.).
2.  **Exploratory Analysis**: Users interact with data through visual interfaces, filters, and AI-assisted topic modeling.
3.  **Pattern Identification**: The platform helps users discover patterns, correlations, and clusters within their data.
4.  **Insight Generation**: Users can then use these insights to inform business strategies, predict outcomes, or understand customer behavior.
5.  **Actionable Outputs**: The insights derived can lead to concrete actions, such as optimizing marketing campaigns or understanding customer churn.

## Ownership
*   **Discovery**: Primarily driven by user interaction with the platform and their own domain expertise, guided by Graphext's exploratory features.
*   **Prioritization**: Influenced by user feedback, market trends (like the rise of AI), and the company's strategic focus on specific use cases (e.g., revenue operations).
*   **Shipping**: Driven by the product team's development cycle, incorporating user needs and technological advancements.
*   **Sales Input**: The enterprise model involves direct customer engagement, providing valuable input on use cases and needs.
*   **Design**: Strong emphasis on user experience and creating a "flow" state, inspired by products like Carto and the principles of Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi.

## Evidence
*   *The principle that seems most basic for making a good product is that there must be a balance between breadth, which is called breadth-first search, and depth.*
*   *The main pain point we saw was that many people wanted to explore data, wanted to have much more autonomy, and they weren't technical people.*
