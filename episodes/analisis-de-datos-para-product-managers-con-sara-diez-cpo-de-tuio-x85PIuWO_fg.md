## Thesis
Tuio (home insurtech) starts data strategy with qualitative insight and whatever spreadsheet exists — instrument later; early product decisions don’t wait on a perfect warehouse.

## Context — Sara Diez, CPO at Tuio, an insurtech company offering home insurance, discusses her product leadership journey and data strategies. Tuio aims to revolutionize traditional insurance with transparency and digitalization, celebrating 20,000 customers after two years.

## Operating loop —
*   Talk to everyone to understand their vision and needs.
*   Identify accessible data sources (e.g., Excel behind a prototype).
*   Match business objectives with available data.
*   Instrument applications for user behavior tracking.
*   Analyze funnels and user journeys.
*   Iterate based on data and user feedback.

## Ownership —
*   Discovery: Primarily product, through client and prospect feedback, and data analysis.
*   Prioritization: Product, balancing stakeholder requests with impact.
*   Shipping: Product, with engineering support.
*   Sales Input: Supported by product through understanding client needs and market trends.
*   Design: Supported by product, ensuring user experience aligns with data insights.

## Evidence —
*   *The most important thing is to understand how to extract intelligence from the data.*
*   *Less is more when it comes to onboarding.*

## Gaps —
*   The specific metrics Tuio uses to measure success beyond customer acquisition cost and churn reduction are not detailed.
*   The technical implementation details of their data infrastructure and specific tools beyond general categories like analytics platforms are not elaborated upon.
*   The interview does not deeply explore how product decisions are made in relation to the company's stage of growth beyond the initial focus on sustainable growth.
