## Thesis
The Hero Camp builds PM education like a product — courses iterate from student outcomes; practical product-first mentality over theory catalogs.


## Context
The Hero Camp is an ecosystem built around product management, offering a school for training, services for companies, and a recruitment agency specializing in product talent. They cater to individuals with 7-8 years of experience looking to advance their careers and companies seeking specialized product talent.

## Operating loop
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Identify Market Need for Product Skills] --> B{Develop Course Curriculum};
    B --> C[Deliver Intensive Training (70 hours)];
    C --> D[Gather Student Feedback & Iterative Improvement];
    D --> A;
    E[Offer Recruitment Services] --> A;
    F[Provide Corporate Consulting] --> A;
```

## Ownership
- Discovery: Yosu and the team continuously research market needs and student feedback to refresh programs.
- Prioritization: The team prioritizes course innovation, aiming for 30% innovation per edition.
- Shipping: Courses are delivered in intensive, bootcamp-style formats, with a strong emphasis on practical application.
- Sales-input: Direct feedback from students and corporate clients informs course development.
- Design: The product designer track is a specific offering, with a focus on bridging UX and product design.

## Evidence
- *The Hero Camp is an ecosystem because we started with a school but quickly created a division that provides services to companies.*
- *Our obsession is that people give us very high ratings, that the professors are passionate and approachable, and that people click during the class.*
