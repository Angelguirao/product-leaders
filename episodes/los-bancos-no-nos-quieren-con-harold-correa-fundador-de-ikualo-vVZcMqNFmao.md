## Thesis
Ikualo builds fintech around migrants banks reject — product is access and trust in a community the incumbents underserve.


## Context
Ikualo is a fintech company that offers banking and money transfer services specifically for migrants. The company aims to address the exclusion faced by this demographic from conventional banking systems, recognizing the significant economic contribution of migrants to Europe.

## Operating loop
The core operating loop for Ikualo involves:
*   Identifying unmet financial needs of migrants (e.g., opening bank accounts with a passport).
*   Developing compliant technological solutions to address these needs.
*   Leveraging a pre-existing community of migrants for early adoption and feedback.
*   Expanding product offerings based on community needs and market opportunities.
*   Utilizing data and AI for credit scoring and personalized financial products.

## Ownership
*   **Discovery:** Primarily driven by the founder's personal experience as a migrant and the needs identified within the migrant community.
*   **Prioritization:** Based on addressing critical pain points for migrants and expanding the ecosystem of services.
*   **Shipping:** The company has faced significant challenges, including a major processor failure that blocked client accounts, requiring them to build in-house solutions.
*   **Sales-input:** Directly from the large, engaged migrant community.
*   **Design:** Supported by the founding team, who are programmers, and the community's feedback.

## Evidence
*   *The world of banking for migrants moves 17.5 billion euros in Europe annually.*
*   *We are the first financial center for migrants in Europe that allows them to empower themselves to change their economic life.*
