## Thesis
Eva shipped adolescent sexual-health chat in Uruguay as a days-long Dialogflow MVP — public-health problem first, conversational AI as the distribution channel for teens.


## Context
Cristina Santamarina is an experienced product leader with a background in AI and digital product development. She founded an AI chatbot named Eva in 2016 to address adolescent pregnancies in Uruguay, which reached over 10,000 users and was presented to the Uruguayan Ministry of Health.

## Operating loop
*   **Problem Identification:** Noticed high rates of unintended adolescent pregnancies in Uruguay.
*   **Prototyping:** Developed an AI chatbot (Eva) in three days using no-code tools and Dialogflow.
*   **User Engagement:** Launched Eva to provide information on contraceptives and detect user location for nearby pharmacy recommendations.
*   **Iterative Improvement:** Expanded Eva's capabilities to include information on periods and endometriosis based on user queries.
*   **Exploration:** Introduced "mood bits" to track user feelings and correlate them with activities, though lacked technical capacity for full model development.
*   **Monetization Attempt:** Explored business models for Eva but focused on learning and user engagement.

## Ownership
*   **Discovery:** User conversations and identified public health problems.
*   **Prioritization:** User-driven, adding content like endometriosis information based on repeated queries.
*   **Shipping:** Manual content creation and chatbot training.
*   **Sales-input:** Direct presentation to the Ministry of Health.
*   **Design:** Eva's persona and conversational flow.

## Evidence
*   *I mounted a chatbot in three days and tried to launch it there.*
*   *Eva never had a business model because everything I tried to do, I couldn't validate.*
