## Thesis
SaferLayer rode a viral document-processing wedge — product proof was throughput of real docs, not pitch decks.


## Context
Carlos Sánchez, Head of Digital Product at Real Madrid, discusses the unique challenges and opportunities of developing digital products for a global sports and entertainment brand with a massive, passionate audience. The club aims to compete for user attention not just with other sports entities but also with entertainment giants like Netflix.

## Operating loop
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Fan & Member Data Analysis] --> B{Identify Value Opportunities};
    B --> C[Develop Digital Products (Content, Commerce, Physical Experience Enhancement)];
    C --> D[Launch & Iterate Products];
    D --> A;
```

## Ownership
- **Discovery**: Primarily driven by fan and member data, with input from various business areas.
- **Prioritization**: A global objective is set, with individual teams owning their specific goals and contributing to the overall vision.
- **Shipping**: Teams own their respective product areas.
- **Sales-input**: Input from all business areas, including marketing, ticketing, and membership.
- **Design**: Integrated within product teams, with a focus on user experience and value delivery.
