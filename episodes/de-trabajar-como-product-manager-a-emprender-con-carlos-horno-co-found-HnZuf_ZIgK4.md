## Thesis
Dealcar verticalizes the whole used-car dealer stack into one OS — inventory, WhatsApp CRM, e-sign, payments — so dealers can sell online without stitching ten tools.


## Context
Dealcar offers a comprehensive software solution for car dealerships, enabling them to sell cars entirely online. The company targets both independent and official dealerships, aiming to transform traditional, paper-based operations into modern, digital-first businesses.

## Operating loop
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Dealer Management System (DMS)] --> B(CRM with WhatsApp Integration);
    B --> C(Website Builder);
    C --> D(Transaction Platform: E-signature, Payment Links);
    D --> E(Financing & Insurance Integration);
    E --> F(Multi-channel Listing & Inventory Management);
    F --> A;
```

## Ownership
- Discovery: Supported by extensive customer interviews (over 500) and mystery shopping.
- Prioritization: Driven by the goal of verticalizing the entire car sales value chain.
- Shipping: Handled by a highly skilled engineering team focused on rapid product development.
- Sales-input: Integrated via CRM and direct dealer interactions.
- Design: The platform itself is the product, with a focus on a seamless user experience.

## Evidence
- *Dealcar aims to be the operating system for car dealers, enabling them to sell 100% online.*
- *The goal is to sell more, sell better, buy more, buy better.*

## Gaps
- The full integration of financing and insurance offerings is still in early stages.
- Strategies for directly addressing the supply side (acquiring more cars for dealerships) are still being developed.
- The platform's ability to fully replicate the seamless, three-click car buying experience is not yet complete.
