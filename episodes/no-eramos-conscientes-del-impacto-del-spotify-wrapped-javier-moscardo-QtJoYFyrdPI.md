## Thesis
Spotify re-centered product strategy at VP level after squad autonomy — cohesion and AI/content breadth beat pure team independence at scale.


## Context
Spotify is a global music and podcast streaming service that has grown significantly since its inception. The company operates at a scale where organizational structure is critical for managing diverse content offerings and technological advancements like AI.

## Operating loop
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[User Feedback & Market Trends] --> B{Hypothesis Generation};
    B --> C{Strategy Definition (VP Level)};
    C --> D{Product Development & Experimentation};
    D --> E{Data Analysis & Iteration};
    E --> A;
```

## Ownership
*   **Discovery:** Primarily owned by VPs and their teams, with a focus on understanding user needs and market shifts.
*   **Prioritization:** Driven by strategic goals set at the VP level, balancing innovation with user experience.
*   **Shipping:** Teams are empowered to ship, but with an emphasis on a cohesive user experience, suggesting centralized oversight for major changes.
*   **Sales-input:** Not explicitly detailed, but likely integrated through business and content leadership.
*   **Design:** Owned by the VP of Experience, with significant autonomy to define user interface and experience strategies.

## Evidence
*   *The internet sort of started with curation of the user curation... And I think what we're entering now is we're going from your curation to recommendation to generation.*
*   *The idea with the with the squad specifically was twofold. They were supposed to be small and sort of full stacks... And And it should be very autonomous was the idea. And And that's really what we shifted.*
