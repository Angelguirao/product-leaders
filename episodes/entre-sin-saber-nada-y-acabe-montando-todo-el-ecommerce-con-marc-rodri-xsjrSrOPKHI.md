## Thesis
Freshly Cosmetics runs vertical integration end-to-end — lab, factory, pack, and DTC — so ecommerce can change formulation and channel without waiting on suppliers.


## Context
Freshly Cosmetics is a digital-native e-commerce company founded in 2016, specializing in natural and sustainable cosmetic products. Initially operating solely online, the company has expanded to include 18 physical stores across Spain and has ventured into wholesale and marketplace sales.

## Operating loop
The company's operating loop involves:
- Product formulation and testing in their own laboratory.
- Manufacturing and packaging at their factory.
- Direct sales through their e-commerce platform and physical stores.
- Marketing and customer acquisition via social media and influencer collaborations.
- Continuous platform migration and optimization, currently moving from PrestaShop to Shopify Plus.
- Customer feedback integration for product and process improvements.

## Ownership
- **Discovery:** Product team, with input from e-commerce and customer feedback.
- **Prioritization:** Product team, in collaboration with business needs.
- **Shipping:** Managed internally, with a focus on optimizing logistics and delivery.
- **Sales Input:** Integrated through physical store performance and online customer data.
- **Design:** Primarily handled by the product team, with a focus on maintaining brand consistency across channels.

## Evidence
- *We control the entire process from start to finish.*
- *We have a very high repeat customer rate, over 80% in some segments.*
