## Thesis
Decathlon Outdoor uses the app to reposition from selling gear to enabling outdoor experiences — product marketing owns lifestyle, not only SKU pages.


## Context
Decathlon Outdoor is a digital product within the large French sports retail company Decathlon. The app focuses on hiking and cycling routes, aiming to inspire users to engage in outdoor activities and enhance their daily lives.

## Operating loop
*   User creates or finds a route.
*   GPS guides user on the route.
*   App tracks activity (distance, time).
*   User earns loyalty points.
*   Data informs product development and store strategy.
