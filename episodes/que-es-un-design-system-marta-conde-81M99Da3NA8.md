## Thesis
A design system's effectiveness hinges on its integration with development and its ability to adapt to a product's evolving needs, rather than being a static set of components.

## Context
Marta Conde discusses the practicalities and evolution of design systems, particularly in the context of digital product development. The conversation touches upon the challenges and benefits of implementing design systems, the impact of AI on design roles, and the broader landscape of the design community in Spain.

## Operating loop
*   **Initial Product Stage:** Rely on existing design libraries for agility.
*   **Growth Stage:** Identify limitations of external libraries for custom needs (e.g., dark mode, custom branding).
*   **System Development:** Build a custom design system with UI kits, guides, and documentation.
*   **Integration:** Synchronize Figma tokens with development repositories.
*   **Iteration & Maintenance:** Continuously refine, add, or remove components, and update documentation.
*   **AI Impact:** Explore AI's role in accelerating prototyping and design workflows.

## Ownership
*   **Discovery:** Primarily driven by product needs and user feedback.
*   **Prioritization:** Managed through communication between design, development, and product teams.
*   **Shipping:** Coordinated between design and development, with a focus on synchronization.
*   **Sales Input:** Not explicitly detailed.
*   **Design:** Owned by the design team, with a focus on creating reusable components and maintaining consistency.

## Evidence
*   *A design system is like an ecosystem that unites a UI kit, guides, and documentation.*
*   *The figure of the Design System appears when the product starts to really work and you see it growing a lot, then you need someone to organize everything you are creating.*

## Gaps
*   Specific details on how sales input directly influences design system decisions are not provided.
*   The exact metrics used to measure the success or ROI of a design system are not elaborated upon.
*   The long-term strategy for maintaining and evolving the design system beyond initial implementation is not fully detailed.
